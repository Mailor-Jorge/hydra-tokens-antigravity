# CONTRIBUTING TO HYDRA TOKENS ANTIGRAVITY

Thank you for contributing! To maintain legal integrity and quality standards, all contributions must follow this process.

---

## 1. Contributor License Agreement (CLA) Requirement
All contributors must agree to the terms of `CLA.md` (for individuals) or `CCLA.md` (for corporate entities).
When opening a pull request, you will be prompted to check the CLA acceptance declaration.

---

## 2. Commit & Pull Request Standards
1. Use semantic commit messages:
   - `feat(...)`, `fix(...)`, `docs(...)`, `chore(...)`, `perf(...)`
2. Never submit third-party code without proper attribution and compatible licensing.
3. Keep diffs minimal and focused (adhering to HYDRA Head-5 formatting standards).
