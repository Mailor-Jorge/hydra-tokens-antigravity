## 2. Who Needs a Commercial License?

You require a commercial license if your organization:
1. Embeds or links HYDRA modules within a closed-source proprietary platform.
2. Provides remote network access to a modified version of the software without disclosing corresponding source code under AGPL-3.0.
3. Desires formal enterprise indemnification, commercial warranty, or enterprise technical support.
