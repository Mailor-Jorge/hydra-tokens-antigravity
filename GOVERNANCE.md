---

## 1. Distribution of Authority

| Authority Domain | Designated Authority | Responsibilities |
|------------------|----------------------|------------------|
| **Project Ownership** | Mailor-Jorge | Overall vision, copyright title, repository ownership |
| **Release Authority** | Mailor-Jorge | Signing tags, approving baseline releases, npm distribution |
| **Security Authority** | Mailor-Jorge | Handling vulnerability disclosures, secret incident response |
| **Licensing & Commercial** | Mailor-Jorge | Commercial dual-licensing negotiations and agreements |
| **Contribution Acceptance**| Mailor-Jorge | Reviewing and merging pull requests, verifying CLA |

---

## 2. Emergency Authority
In the event of a critical security vulnerability, credential compromise, or legal notice:
- The Project Lead reserves the right to freeze the repository, revert commits, revoke compromised release tags, or issue emergency security patches immediately.

---

## 3. Succession and Continuity
Should the primary maintainer be unable to continue governance, authority shall transition in accordance with explicit testamentary instructions or be transferred to a designated successor registered in repository metadata.
